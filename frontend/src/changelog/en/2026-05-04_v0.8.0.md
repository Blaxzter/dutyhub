## Pick One Event to Work With

WirkSam now centres your view around a single active event. The first time you sign in, a new full-screen picker asks which event you want to focus on; from then on the app shows only that event's tasks, shifts, and bookings until you choose to switch. The "Change event" button on your settings page and a dedicated picker entry in the mobile menu both bring you back to the same screen.

![Select-event picker with hero pane and event cards on the right](./images/select-event.png)

## A Warmer Default Theme

The app ships with a new earthy "Default" palette — cream backgrounds, forest-green accents, terracotta highlights — designed to feel less clinical for the volunteer-coordination context. The original monochrome shadcn look is preserved as "Classic" and is one click away. Light, dark, and system mode each work with both palettes.

![Appearance settings showing Default and Classic theme tiles plus Light/Dark/System mode picker](./images/appearance.png)

You'll also find a quick Appearance button in the public-page header so you can preview either palette before signing in.

## Notifications, Reorganized

The notification inbox has been redesigned around how notifications actually feel: classification pills at the top let you flip between Reminders, Updates, Matches, and Announcements; mark-read and dismiss buttons are always visible (no more menus); and on touch devices you can swipe a row to read or dismiss it. Each row expands inline to show its action target. Skeleton loaders replace the empty flash on first open.

![Redesigned notifications inbox with classification tabs and inline action buttons](./images/notifications-redesign.png)

## Availability Without the Dialog

Setting your availability used to open a dialog stacked on top of the page; it now lives directly on the Availability view. Pick "Fully available", "Daily window", or "Specific times", paint your hours into the grid, and use the Save button at the top to commit. A new team-availability heatmap below shows at a glance who else is around on which days, with a clearer "less / full" legend.

![My Availability page with mode picker, paint grid, and team availability heatmap](./images/availability-editor.png)

## Self-Hosted Profile Pictures

Profile pictures are no longer fetched from your identity provider. Upload any PNG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF (first frame); WirkSam crops, resizes, and stores a small WebP locally, so changing your picture works the same regardless of how you signed up. The Profile screen has a new picker with crop and rotate controls, and avatar updates appear instantly across the app.

![Profile settings with the new avatar picker and updated sidebar (Active Event, Appearance, etc.)](./images/profile-avatar.png)

## Smaller Touches

- New **Active Event** and **Appearance** sections in user settings.
- Added a **Time format** preference (12-hour vs. 24-hour) saved to your profile.
- Optional **sidebar event switcher**: turn it on under Settings → Active Event to get a quick switcher card at the top of the sidebar; off by default.
- Mobile onboarding now slides between intro, event picker, and notification setup as a single horizontal flow rather than swapping panels in place.
- Existing event groups, events, and tasks were unaffected — only the way you navigate between them has changed.
