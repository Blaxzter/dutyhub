## Signing In and Joining, Redrawn

The sign-in and registration screens used to be a small box floating in the
middle of an empty page. They are now a proper two-panel screen: what WirkSam is
for on one side, and nothing but the form on the other.

![The sign-in screen, with the roster filling up beside the form](./images/sign-in.png)

The panel on the left is not decoration for its own sake — it shows the thing
the app is actually about. Days across, shifts down, and the places on them
filling up, which is what your event looks like once your team gets going.

## Creating an Account

![Creating an account, with the password typed twice](./images/create-account.png)

Two things are different beyond the look.

- **You type your password twice.** A typo in the one field you cannot read back
  used to mean finding out at your next sign-in. The second field catches it
  while you are still on the page.
- **The password rule is stated once.** There is a single line under the field
  that shows what is needed, ticks over when you have met it, and turns into the
  problem if there is one — instead of the same sentence printed twice, once as
  a hint and once as a complaint.

## The Forms Speak Your Language

If you had WirkSam in German and left a field empty, it used to answer
"Invalid input" — in English, in the middle of a German page. Every message a
form can give you is now written in both languages, and it says something
useful: an empty box asks to be filled in rather than being called invalid.

Errors are also finally the colour of an error. They were being drawn in the
same colour as ordinary text, which is a poor way to tell somebody something
went wrong.

## Less Shouting While You Type

The forms used to go red as you typed the first letter of your email address —
telling you that you were wrong before you had finished being right. They now
wait until you leave a field before saying anything. Once a field is showing a
problem, it does keep watching, so the message clears the moment you fix it.

## On a Phone

![Creating an account on a phone](./images/create-account-mobile.png)

The panel folds down to a slim band with the logo in it, so the first field is
where your thumb already is rather than below a screen of introduction.

## Smaller Touches

- The privacy, terms and imprint links, the language switch and the light/dark
  switch are all on the sign-in screen now. You can change the language before
  signing in, which matters if you cannot read the page you are looking at.
- Submitting a form with something missing puts the cursor in the first field
  that needs you, rather than leaving you to hunt for it.
- The show-password eye sits inside its field instead of on top of its border.
- The dismiss buttons on the cookie notice were being swallowed by an invisible
  layer over them. They work.
- Nothing about your events, shifts or bookings changed. As with the last
  release, this one is about the way in.
