## Brown Was Never the Plan

WirkSam's light theme is warm on purpose — cream paper, forest green, a little
terracotta. The dark theme was made by taking those same colours and turning
the lights down, which sounds sensible and is not. Warmth at full brightness
reads as paper. The same warmth at a tenth of it reads as mud, and so every
card, every sidebar and every panel in the app carried a brown cast that nobody
had chosen.

![The dashboard, as it was](./images/dark-theme-before.png)

The dark theme is now its own thing rather than a dimmed copy of the light one.
Its greys lean very slightly green instead of very slightly orange, which
leaves the green itself as the only real colour on screen.

![The dashboard, now](./images/dark-theme-after.png)

The light theme has not changed. Cream is still cream.

## Where the Warm Ink Went

A few small things were written in terracotta rather than merely sitting on it:
the two handles you drag to set your hours on the availability screen, and
whichever section you have open in settings. Terracotta text is a pleasant
colour and a poor one to read, so those are plain text now — dark on the light
theme, light on the dark one.

The two theme previews in your settings also tell the truth again. Each one
shows a dot in its palette's own signal colour, and on the black-and-white
theme that dot had been a pale grey on a white card — technically present,
practically invisible.
