## Try It Before You Sign Up

Until now the only way to find out what WirkSam is like was to make an account,
make an event, and fill it with shifts — a fair amount of work to do before you
know whether the thing suits you at all.

There is a third button on the front page now: **Try a demo event**. One click
and you are inside a working event, with jobs, a schedule, a few volunteers and
some shifts already taken. No account, no email address, nothing to fill in.

![Choosing which side of a demo event to see](./images/demo-start.png)

The event is made fresh for you when you press the button, and it is dated
around today — so the calendar has this week in it, some shifts have already
happened, and some are still waiting for someone.

## Two Sides, Because There Are Two Jobs

WirkSam is used by two kinds of people, and they see different things. So the
demo asks which one you are.

**As a helper** you get the volunteer's view: find the shifts that need someone,
put your name on one, say which days you are free, and check what you have
signed up for.

**As an organiser** you get the other half: create a job, generate a whole
schedule of shifts from it in one go, see at a glance which slots are still
empty, handle who is in your event, and read how it went afterwards.

You can start a second demo as the other side whenever you like.

## Somebody Shows You Around

![The guided tour, partway through the organiser's side](./images/demo-tour.png)

The demo does not just drop you in and wish you luck. A short guided tour walks
you through the screens in the order you would actually use them, one step at a
time, moving you between pages as it goes — nine steps for the helper's side,
thirteen for the organiser's.

You can leave the tour at any point and simply poke around instead. The bar
along the top has a **Restart tour** button for when you want it back.

## It Tidies Itself Up

![The bar along the top, counting down](./images/demo-banner.png)

The demo lasts an hour. The bar at the top counts down, and when the time runs
out the event and everything in it is deleted — the shifts, the bookings, the
made-up volunteers, and the temporary account it all belonged to.

If you are done before then, **Exit demo** removes it straight away. Either way
nothing is kept, nothing is shared with anyone else, and nothing you do in there
touches a real event. It is genuinely impossible to break anything.

Nobody but you can see your demo. It does not turn up in the list of events to
discover, it is not something anyone can be invited to, and — the one thing
worth saying plainly — the demo cannot send email. The organiser's tour visits
the invitation screen and shows you how it works, but the send button is off.

## Running Your Own WirkSam

The demo is on by default. If you host WirkSam yourself and would rather not
have it, `SANDBOX_ENABLED=false` switches it off, and the button stops being
offered on the front page. There are two more settings for how long a demo
lasts and how many can run at once; both have sensible defaults and most
installations will never need to touch them.
