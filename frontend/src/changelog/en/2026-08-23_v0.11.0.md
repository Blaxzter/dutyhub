## Sign In Stays In WirkSam

Signing in used to hand you over to a different website. You clicked "Sign in",
the address bar changed to somewhere that was not WirkSam, you typed your
password there, and it sent you back.

Now the sign-in form is simply a page in WirkSam, in your language, in your
theme. You never leave, and the address bar never changes to something you did
not expect.

## Creating an Account

Registration asks for your name, your email address and a password, and that is
the whole form. You are signed in the moment it is done — no confirmation step
standing between you and the app.

A message goes to your address so you can confirm it. You do not have to act on
it before you start using WirkSam; it is there so we know the address is really
yours.

## Forgot Your Password

There is a "Forgot your password?" link under the sign-in form. Give it your
address and it sends you a link that lets you choose a new password. The link
works once and expires after an hour.

For your safety, the page says the same thing whether or not it recognises the
address. Someone who is not you cannot use it to find out who has an account
here.

## Your Password and Your Devices

**Settings → Security** is a real section now, rather than a link out to another
site.

- **Change your password.** You confirm the one you use now, choose a new one,
  and every other device you are signed in on is signed out. The tab you are
  working in stays as it is.
- **See where you are signed in.** Each device shows what it is, when it signed
  in, and where from — with the one you are using marked. Anything you do not
  recognise can be signed out on its own.

## Your Profile Is Yours Again

Your display name, nickname and short bio used to live on the other service.
They are stored with the rest of your account now, which is why they save the
same way everything else does.

## Smaller Touches

- Following a link while signed out takes you to sign in and then on to the page
  you actually wanted, instead of dropping you on the home screen.
- Signing in and reloading keep you signed in. Closing the tab and coming back
  the next day does too.
- Passwords are at least 8 characters. If you use umlauts or emoji, a very long
  one may be refused — the form tells you rather than failing quietly.
- Repeated sign-in attempts from the same place are slowed down after a while.
- Nothing about your events, shifts or bookings changed. This release is about
  how you get in.
