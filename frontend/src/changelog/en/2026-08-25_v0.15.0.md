## The Dashboard Answers Something Now

The screen you land on used to be a month calendar with everything painted onto
it at once — your bookings, the jobs, the event itself, all in the same grid of
coloured bars. It looked like an overview. It was not one.

Two questions bring people to that screen. *Am I due anywhere?* and *where are
people still missing?* Neither could be read off the calendar. Both meant
opening days one at a time and counting.

So the calendar is gone from the home screen, and the dashboard says those two
things out loud instead.

![The dashboard, for somebody who both runs an event and helps at it](./images/home-overview.png)

## Your Next Shift, In Big Letters

![The next shift you are on](./images/home-next-shift.png)

The largest thing on the page is the one fact you were checking: when to turn
up. Under it, in this order, is everything you would want on the way out of the
door — the day, which job it is, where to go, and who else will be there.

If you have nothing booked, the card does not simply say so and stop. It turns
into the offer instead: how many places are still waiting for somebody, and a
button that takes you to them.

## Where People Are Still Missing

![The shifts that still have room, soonest first](./images/home-needs-people.png)

Every shift in your event that still has room, soonest first. Each one carries
its time, its place and how much of it is left — **1 place left**, **3 places
left**, or **Nobody yet** for the ones no one has touched at all.

Pressing any of them opens the shift, so you can take it without leaving the
page. Shifts you are already on are left out; they are on the other list, and
offering them to you again would just be strange.

Underneath your next shift, the rest of what you have said yes to is listed the
same way, with the hours it adds up to.

## If You Are Running The Event

![The organiser's list of things waiting to be dealt with](./images/home-attention.png)

Organisers get one more block, at the very top: the things this week that are
waiting on a decision from you. Shifts in the next seven days with nobody on
them, shifts still short of people, anyone asking to join, and jobs still
sitting in draft.

Each line is a link to the screen where you would do something about it. When
there is nothing to report the block is not there at all — an empty "needs your
attention" panel just teaches people to stop reading it.

If you only help out at an event, none of this appears for you.

## On A Phone

![The dashboard on a phone](./images/home-mobile.png)

The same page, in one column, in the same order: what needs you, when you are
next due, what is left to cover. No sideways scrolling, no calendar grid
squeezed into 390 pixels.

## The Calendar Is Still There

It just lives where it is useful. **Tasks** has had a month, week and day view
all along, with the filters and the date navigation that make a calendar worth
having. Nothing about it changed — it simply stopped being the first thing you
see.

One last thing, for anyone with a foot in more than one event: the shifts you
have booked are listed across *all* of them, whichever event the switcher is
pointing at, and any that belong somewhere else say so. A duty you promised to
turn up to should not go quiet because you were looking at something else.
