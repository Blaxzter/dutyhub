## The Public Page, Rebuilt

Everything an unregistered visitor sees has been rewritten. The old page led
with a logo and three vague cards, then a carousel of eight screenshots you had
to click through — and its text still described a WirkSam where an administrator
had to set your event up for you.

The page now opens on what the product actually is: a roster board showing three
days of shifts filling up, next to a plain description of what WirkSam does. The
carousel is gone.

## One Page Instead of Three

**About** and **How It Works** were separate pages that each repeated a slice of
the same story. They are now sections of the landing page, reached by scrolling
or from the header. The old addresses still work — they land on the matching
section — so any link you have shared stays good.

A floating bar appears once you scroll past the top, showing which section you
are in and jumping to any other.

## How It Works, From Both Sides

The old page only ever explained one thing: the shift-generation wizard. It said
nothing about creating an event, inviting anyone, or what taking part looks like.

Both journeys are now laid out end to end, four steps each, and you pick the side
you are on:

- **Organising** — create the event, invite your people, generate the shifts,
  publish and watch it fill.
- **Helping out** — accept the invitation, say when you are free, book your
  shifts, get reminded.

## Screenshots That Match What You See

Every screenshot on the page was recaptured, and they now follow your theme:
light captures in light mode, dark in dark. Click any of them to open it full
size, page between them with the arrow keys, and close with Escape.

The German screenshots were two releases out of date — still showing the old
monochrome look and a "pending users" queue that no longer exists — and three of
them were missing entirely, leaving broken images for German visitors. Both
languages are now complete and current.

## Smaller Touches

- The public pages scroll normally again. They used to be a fixed header over an
  inner scrolling box, which broke anchor links and the browser's own scroll
  restoration.
- Jumping to a section glides rather than teleporting, and respects your system
  setting if you have asked for reduced motion.
- No more burger menu on phones. The public page is one scrolling document, so
  the menu only added a step between you and the sign-in button, which now sits
  in the bar itself.
- The footer's legal links wrap instead of running off the edge of a narrow
  screen.
- Nothing about your account, events, or bookings changed — this release only
  touches the pages you see before signing in.
