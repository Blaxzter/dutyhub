## Anyone Can Start an Event

You no longer have to ask an administrator to set an event up for you. The
"Create event" button on the event picker is there for everyone, and whoever
creates an event owns it: you edit it, add tasks and shifts, and decide who
takes part.

## You Decide Who Joins

Each event now has its own list of people, on a new **People** tab in the event
settings. Everyone taking part appears there with their role:

- **Owner** — created the event, or had it handed over. Can delete it and hand
  it on.
- **Organiser** — runs the event alongside the owner: tasks, shifts, members,
  invitations.
- **Member** — takes part and books shifts.

Owners and organisers can promote someone, remove someone, or leave the event
themselves. The owner cannot be removed, so an event never ends up with nobody
in charge — hand it over first, and you stay on as an organiser.

## Invitations, by Email or by Link

Invite people by pasting one address or a whole list, and pick whether they
join as an organiser or a member. Addresses that are already taking part are
reported back and skipped rather than failing the batch.

Prefer a link? Create a share link instead and send it however you like — it
stays usable until you withdraw it. Email invitations are single-use and go to
the address you named; anyone opening one meant for someone else is turned
away.

Either way the invitee gets a page showing the event, who invited them, and the
role they would take, before they decide.

## Private by Default, Public if You Want

A new event is **private**: only people you invite can find it or join. Switch
it to **public** and it appears under a new **Discover** tab on the event
picker, where anyone signed in can ask to join. Requests land on the People tab
and the event's own organisers approve or decline them — nobody at WirkSam sits
in the middle.

## Signing Up No Longer Means Waiting

New accounts used to sit in a queue until an administrator approved them. That
queue is gone: you can sign in straight away. An account on its own shows you
nothing, though — you see an event once you create one, accept an invitation,
or are let into a public one. The first screen after signing up opens on
Discover, with recommended events first.

## Smaller Touches

- **Recommended events.** Events picked out by a WirkSam administrator appear
  at the top of Discover, so a brand-new account has somewhere to start.
- **Manage Events** is now **My Events**, and it lists the events you run
  rather than being administrator-only.
- **Reports** are open to anyone who owns or organises an event, covering the
  events they run.
- The home screen's old "pending users" tile now counts join requests waiting
  on you.
- Nothing was lost in the move: your existing events stayed visible to everyone
  who could already see them, the people who managed an event became its
  organisers, and anyone who had booked a shift or entered their availability
  is now a member of that event.
